# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user: React, TypeScript, and Vite for a fast, maintainable single-page application. Data is stored locally in the browser for the first release.

## Users

Primary users are Gen Z students, new graduates, and early-career job seekers managing roughly 5–40 active applications across job boards, referrals, and company sites.

## Product Purpose

Help job seekers keep every opportunity, follow-up, interview, and deadline visible in one calm workspace. Success means users can understand their pipeline in seconds and know the next useful action without maintaining a spreadsheet.

## Positioning

The tracker behaves like a personal job-search cockpit: the pipeline and the next actions share one surface, so progress is organized around momentum rather than database maintenance.

## Operating Context

Users revisit the product several times per week on laptops and phones while applying, preparing for interviews, or following up. They manually add opportunities from any source and move them through a lightweight pipeline.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Dashboard and kanban pipeline with application counts and upcoming actions.
- Add, edit, delete, search, filter, and change status for applications.
- Track company, role, location, work mode, source, salary, link, deadline, next action, notes, and date applied.
- Browser-local persistence with useful demo data and no account requirement.
- No backend, authentication, job-board integrations, email sync, or multi-user collaboration in the first release.
- Inferred from the request because implementation was requested immediately; product naming remains editable.

## Brand Commitments

Modern and relevant to Gen Z, but easy to use and never visually noisy. Voice is concise, encouraging, and practical.

## Evidence on Hand

No customer research, production content, brand assets, or commercial claims were supplied. Any seeded application records are clearly demonstration data.

## Product Principles

- Make the next action more visible than historical detail.
- Reward momentum without gamifying rejection or anxiety.
- Keep data entry lightweight and forgiving.
- Make every important workflow usable on mobile and with a keyboard.
- Prefer clear status language over recruiter jargon.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive web experience with semantic controls, visible focus states, keyboard operation, reduced-motion support, and WCAG-minded color contrast.
